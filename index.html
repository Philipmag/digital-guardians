Think You've Been Scammed?

What to Do Now

  1. Don't panic. These things happen to smart people.
  2. Stop all contact with the person or website.
  3. Call your bank using the number on the back of your card.
  4. Change passwords for any affected accounts.
  5. Report it to help protect others.

Where to Report

  • FTC (USA): reportfraud.ftc.gov
  • Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: antifraudcentre.ca
  • Local Police: Non-emergency line
