Quick Start Guide

New to the internet?

Start with these fundamentals:

  1. Learn to use email

    Email is how you receive messages, bills, and news online.

  2. Create strong passwords

    Your passwords protect your personal information.

  3. Learn to spot scams

    Knowing what to avoid keeps you safe online.

You can do this! Millions of seniors use the internet every day to stay connected with family, manage their finances, and learn new things.

Everyday Tips

🔒

Take Your Time

Never feel rushed to click a link or provide information. Legitimate businesses won't pressure you.

👥

Ask for Help

It's okay to ask a family member or friend to help you check something you're unsure about.

📝

Write It Down

Keep a notebook with your passwords and important information in a safe place at home.

🔍

When in Doubt

If a message seems strange, it probably is. Trust your instincts and don't respond.
